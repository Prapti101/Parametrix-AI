# Parametrix AI – Intelligent Income Protection for Gig Workers

#1. Introduction

India’s gig economy is rapidly growing, with millions of delivery partners working for platforms like Swiggy, Zomato, and Amazon. These workers depend on daily earnings for their livelihood. However, external disruptions such as heavy rainfall, extreme heat, air pollution, and sudden curfews significantly reduce their ability to work, leading to direct income loss.

Currently, there is no structured mechanism that compensates gig workers for such uncontrollable disruptions. This creates financial instability and uncertainty in their day-to-day lives.


#2. Problem Statement

Gig workers frequently experience income loss due to environmental and external factors beyond their control. These disruptions can reduce their monthly earnings by 20–30%. Existing insurance systems do not address this specific problem, as they focus on health, accidents, or asset damage rather than loss of income.

There is a clear need for a system that can:

* Identify real-world disruptions
* Quantify income loss
* Provide quick and reliable compensation


 #3. Proposed Solution

Parametrix AI is an AI-powered parametric insurance platform designed to protect gig workers against income loss. The system automatically detects predefined disruption conditions and triggers payouts without requiring manual claims.

The solution is designed to be simple, fast, and accessible, with a focus on automation and real-time data.


 #4. Target Persona

The primary users of this system are delivery partners working in urban environments.

Example:
A Swiggy delivery partner earns approximately ₹600–₹800 per day. During heavy rainfall or extreme weather conditions, the number of orders drops significantly or operations are halted. As a result, the worker loses a full day’s income.

Parametrix AI ensures that such workers receive partial compensation for these lost earnings.


 #5. System Workflow

The application follows a straightforward workflow:

1. The user registers on the platform and selects a weekly insurance plan.
2. The system continuously monitors real-time environmental data such as weather conditions, air quality, and location.
3. When a disruption exceeds predefined thresholds, the system identifies it as a valid trigger event.
4. The user’s activity and location are verified.
5. A claim is automatically generated.
6. The payout is processed instantly and credited to the user.

This eliminates the need for manual claim submission and approval processes.


 #6. Parametric Triggers

The system relies on predefined parameters to trigger payouts. These include:

* Rainfall exceeding a specific threshold (e.g., >50 mm)
* Temperature exceeding extreme limits (e.g., >40°C)
* Air Quality Index above hazardous levels (e.g., AQI >300)
* External events such as curfews or restricted access zones

These triggers are objective and data-driven, ensuring transparency and fairness.


#7. Weekly Pricing Model

The platform operates on a weekly subscription model to align with the earning patterns of gig workers.

Example plans:

* Basic Plan: ₹30 per week
* Standard Plan: ₹60 per week
* Premium Plan: ₹100 per week

Premium pricing is dynamically adjusted based on:

* Risk level of the user’s operating area
* Historical weather patterns
* User activity and reliability


 #8. AI/ML Integration

Artificial Intelligence plays a key role in enhancing the system’s effectiveness:

## Risk Assessment

The system analyzes historical and real-time environmental data to assess the likelihood of disruptions in specific areas.

## Dynamic Premium Calculation

Premiums are adjusted based on localized risk factors, ensuring fair pricing for users.

## Earnings Prediction

The system estimates potential income loss based on past activity and average earnings.

## Fraud Detection

To prevent misuse, the system incorporates:

* GPS-based location verification
* Activity validation
* Detection of duplicate or abnormal claim patterns


 #9. Key Features

## Micro-Zone Risk Analysis

Instead of using city-level data, the system evaluates risk at a more granular, area-specific level. This improves accuracy in both pricing and payouts.

## Automated Claim Processing

Claims are triggered automatically when predefined conditions are met, eliminating manual intervention.

## Pre-Loss Alerts

Users receive notifications about potential disruptions (e.g., expected heavy rainfall), allowing them to plan their work accordingly.

### Earnings-Based Compensation

Payouts are calculated based on estimated income loss rather than fixed values, making compensation more realistic.

## Trust Score System

Each user is assigned a reliability score based on behavior and claim history, which can influence premium rates.

## Safe Zone Recommendations

The system can suggest lower-risk areas to help users avoid disruptions and maintain earnings.


 #10. Technology Stack
